Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Two features touch personal data at all: the contact form and the arcade leaderboard. This page explains both, and nothing else, because there is nothing else.

1. Who is responsible

This site is operated by Manovishaal D, an individual based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. For any question about this policy or about data held about you, write to manovishaal@gmail.com. I am the point of contact; there is no separate data protection officer, and none is required at this scale.

2. What is collected, and why

FeatureDataWhyKept
Contact form Name, email address, enquiry type, message text So I can read your message and reply to it. Nothing else. In my email inbox for as long as the conversation is useful, then deleted.
Arcade leaderboard A callsign you type in, your score, the level reached, and the time of submission To display a public high-score table for the mini-game. Until you ask me to remove it, or until I reset the board.
Your browser's local storage Your chosen callsign and your theme preference So the game remembers your name and the site remembers light or dark mode. On your own device only. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What is deliberately not collected

No analytics or measurement service. No advertising or marketing pixels. No behavioural profiling. No account system. No newsletter list. No IP-based location lookup. No session recording or heatmaps. If any of that changes, this page changes first.

3. The leaderboard is public — please use a nickname

Whatever you type as a callsign is shown publicly on the high-score table and is readable by anyone who visits the site. It is stored in a hosted database (see section 5). Please pick a nickname rather than your real name, and do not put an email address, phone number or anything else identifying into that field.

The mini-game is a toy on a portfolio site and is not directed at children. It has no ads, no purchases and no messaging. If you are a parent or guardian and your child has submitted a score under a name you would rather not have public, email me and I will delete the row — no verification hoops, just tell me the callsign.

4. Cookies

This site sets no cookies at all — not first-party, not third-party, not analytics, not advertising. That is why you are not being shown a consent banner: under the EU ePrivacy rules and equivalent guidance, consent is required for storing or reading information on your device beyond what you have asked for, and this site stores only your own callsign and theme choice in local storage, both of which exist purely to deliver features you asked for.

If analytics, embedded video, a comments widget or any advertising is ever added, a consent banner becomes necessary and this section must be rewritten before that code ships.

5. Third parties and where data goes

The site loads or sends data to exactly four external services:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts the site. GitHub receives your IP address and browser user-agent as part of serving the page, as any web host does, and handles those under its own privacy statement.
  • Supabase — hosts the leaderboard database (region: Mumbai, India). Receives your callsign, score and level when you submit a score.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces. Your IP address is visible to Google when the fonts are fetched.
  • jsDelivr — serves the JavaScript library the leaderboard uses. Your IP address is visible to that CDN when the file is fetched.

Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for marketing. There are no data brokers, no ad networks and no affiliate tracking anywhere on this site.

6. Legal basis

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies to a visitor, the basis for the contact form is your consent, given by choosing to fill it in and send it, and for the leaderboard your consent, given by choosing to submit a score. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by emailing me. Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies, the basis is likewise your consent for a clearly stated purpose.

7. Your rights

You can ask me to show you what data I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can ask me to remove a leaderboard entry. You can withdraw consent for me to keep an email exchange. Send the request to manovishaal@gmail.com and I will act on it within 30 days — realistically within a couple of days, since the volume here is tiny.

If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. In India, once the relevant provisions of the DPDP Act are in force, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

Traffic to this site is served over HTTPS. The leaderboard database has row-level security enabled and the public key used by the browser can only read the score table and add a new row to it — it cannot read anything else, and cannot edit or delete existing rows.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Material changes to what is collected will be described here rather than quietly folded in.
